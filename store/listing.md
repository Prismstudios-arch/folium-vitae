## Description (4000)

    Point your camera at any plant and Sorrel tells you what it is — then helps you keep it alive.

    IDENTIFY
    Photograph a plant and get its name in seconds, with a confidence score on every answer and the other likely candidates when it is a close call. Sorrel says when it is unsure instead of guessing at you.

    CARE THAT FITS THE SEASON
    Care notes for 136 plants, plus general notes for 90 plant groups: light, water, soil, feeding, repotting, pruning and propagation. Watering advice changes with the time of year where you live, north or south.

    SAFE AROUND PETS AND CHILDREN
    Clear toxicity warnings, with the ASPCA's cat and dog listings cited for every plant where they exist.

    YOUR COLLECTION
    Save a plant and it keeps a photo journal and a watering log. Reminders come from your own watering history rather than a generic schedule, so they fit how your home actually grows things.

    WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
    Every care guide has a troubleshooting section — yellowing leaves, brown tips, drooping — with the likely cause and what to do. Premium adds a photo health check that reads a leaf and suggests what to try first, gentlest options before chemical ones.

    HONEST BY DESIGN
    No account needed. Every identification shows its confidence. Care notes say where they came from, and say plainly when they have not yet been reviewed by a horticulturist. No fake discounts, no countdown timers.

    FREE AND PREMIUM
    Free includes three identifications a day, the whole care library, your collection, journals, watering logs and reminders. Premium removes the daily limit and adds plant health checks. Prices are shown in the app in your own currency before anything starts, and a subscription can be cancelled any time in your Apple account settings.

    Sorrel is a guide, not a substitute for professional advice. Never eat a plant based on an app.

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes" on the version page)

    No account or login is needed — Sorrel works anonymously from first launch.

    To test identification: tap "Scan a plant" on the home screen and photograph
    any plant, or choose a plant photo from the library. Identification needs a
    network connection. Free accounts get three identifications a day.

    To test the subscription: tap the Premium banner in Settings, or "Check a
    plant's health" on the home screen, which is Premium-only. The paywall loads
    its prices from App Store Connect and offers a monthly and an annual plan
    with a one-week free trial. Sandbox purchases unlock both unlimited
    identifications and the health check immediately.

    Camera access is used only to photograph plants, photo library access only
    to pick an existing plant photo. The app does not use tracking.

## App Privacy — answers already given

Data collected and linked to the user: none beyond what the app needs to run.
Photos are sent for identification and not retained for advertising; no tracking
across apps or websites; no third-party advertising SDKs.
